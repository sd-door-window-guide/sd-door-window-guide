Milgard has officially launched the C700, their new fiberglass window and patio door line, and the early reception from the trade has been strong. Fiberglass is a category Milgard has been underrepresented in while competitors in the window space have been building out their fiberglass offerings. The C700 changes that conversation significantly.

If you're speccing it or a client has asked about it, the product looks genuinely compelling. The catch right now: lead times are extended significantly while Milgard works through the production ramp-up.

## What the C700 Is

The C700 is Milgard's entry into the premium fiberglass window and patio door segment — a product category that's been growing in San Diego as homeowners look for the performance advantages of fiberglass over vinyl and aluminum. Key characteristics:

- **Fiberglass construction** — superior dimensional stability vs. vinyl, won't warp or expand with coastal humidity cycles
- **Smooth factory finish** — available in 6 standard colors, finished from the factory (not a wood-grain or textured surface)
- **Field repaintable** — can be sanded and repainted down the road if the client wants to change colors, which vinyl cannot offer
- **Thermally efficient** — improved U-factor and Solar Heat Gain Coefficient specs compared to Milgard's vinyl lines
- **Available in a range of configurations** — casement, double-hung, sliding, awning, fixed, and patio door units
- **Not an entry door product** — the C700 is windows and glass patio doors; it is not a replacement for a fiberglass entry door system

For San Diego specifically, fiberglass performs exceptionally well in coastal microclimates. It doesn't conduct heat the way aluminum does, and it holds up to salt air far better than vinyl in direct exposure situations. The smooth factory finish in 6 standard colors also gives architects and designers clean options that don't require field painting on delivery.

## The Lead Time Reality

Here's the honest situation: Milgard is currently quoting **16 weeks** on C700 orders — roughly three to four times the normal production window they're targeting once the line is fully operational. Normal lead times once production normalizes are expected to be in the **4–6 week** range, which would put the C700 in line with Milgard's other residential lines.

The extended lead time is a result of two things happening simultaneously: high initial demand from dealers and contractors who've been waiting for a Milgard fiberglass window option, and the operational reality of switching over production lines to a new product. Milgard is investing in capacity, but that takes time to come online.

## What This Means for Your Project

**Plan around the 16-week window.** If you're in design development and the C700 is on the radar, get a quote and place an order as early as possible. A 16-week lead time means orders placed today arrive in late November — cutting it close for any project with a year-end completion target.

**Don't spec it for a fast-track job.** If a project has a hard move-in date within the next 3–4 months, the C700 is not the right call right now. In that scenario, Milgard's Tuscany vinyl line or a competitor's fiberglass window product with available stock is the more responsible spec.

**Lead times will improve.** This is a production ramp issue, not a product quality issue. As Milgard normalizes the line, 4–6 weeks is a very competitive lead time for a premium fiberglass product. Clients who can be patient are speccing correctly for the long term.

## The Competitive Position

With the C700, Milgard is now a credible competitor in fiberglass window conversations that previously required going outside their product family. Milgard's distribution network in San Diego is strong — which means warranty service and dealer support will be local, not a mail-order experience.

For architects and designers who've been loyal to Milgard's aluminum and vinyl lines, the C700 now lets them spec fiberglass within the same manufacturer relationship. That's a genuine workflow simplification on mixed-product projects.

The factory-finished smooth surface and 6 standard colors also positions the C700 well for contemporary and modern projects where a clean, consistent exterior color is the goal — no field painting, no grain texture, just a uniform factory finish that holds up.

## Bottom Line

The C700 is the real deal — a product the market has been asking Milgard to make for years. The 16-week lead time is a short-term growing pain on a long-term good product. Order early, communicate the timeline clearly to your clients, and plan accordingly.
