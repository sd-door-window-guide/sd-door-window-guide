Milgard has notified dealers of a 5–7% price increase taking effect September 1, 2025, affecting most Trinsic, Tuscany, and Style Line product lines. The increase follows similar moves by other major manufacturers responding to sustained aluminum and vinyl resin cost pressures.

## What's Going Up

The increase applies broadly across:

- **Trinsic Series** (aluminum) — expect 6–7% on most sizes
- **Tuscany Series** (vinyl) — 5–6% depending on configuration
- **Style Line Series** (vinyl) — 5% across the board
- **Moving Glass Wall systems** — being quoted separately, contact your rep

The Ultra series (fiberglass) is not included in this round.

## What It Means for Active Bids

If you're a contractor with an active Milgard quote, check your expiration date carefully. Most current quotes expire 30 days from issue — anything dated before August 1st may not be honored at the old price after September 1st. Get your orders in before end of August if you're under budget pressure.

## San Diego Market Impact

San Diego distributors — including OrepAC and American Building Supply — are typically absorbing 1–2 weeks of order volume before a price increase as contractors rush to lock in pricing. Lead times on Trinsic sliders are currently running 3–4 weeks, so order now if you want delivery before the increase hits.

## The Alternative Conversation

A price increase is always a good time to revisit the spec. If a client is price-sensitive and currently specced for Milgard Trinsic, this is a natural moment to introduce the Simonton Impressions or Western Window Systems comparison — both of which have been stable on pricing through Q3.
