Fleetwood has notified dealers of a 5–7% price increase taking effect in September 2026, affecting most of their residential and commercial product lines. This includes their widely-specified multi-slide, folding, and window systems — the products that show up on virtually every high-end contemporary build in San Diego.

## What's Going Up

The increase applies across Fleetwood's core residential lineup:

- **Fleetwood Multi-Slide doors** — 5–7% depending on configuration and size
- **Folding door systems** — 6–7% on larger opening configurations
- **Window wall systems** — 5–6%
- **Pivot door systems (Series 4400-T)** — 7% on most configurations
- **Aluminum window systems** — 5–6% across the board

Fleetwood has not published specific exemptions — assume the increase is broad unless your dealer confirms otherwise.

## Why It's Happening

Fleetwood's pricing has been relatively stable through early 2026. The September increase reflects ongoing pressure on extruded aluminum costs, freight from their California manufacturing operation, and hardware component pricing — particularly on the European-sourced multipoint lock systems used across their door lineup.

This is not unusual for the fall cycle. Fleetwood, LaCantina, and NanaWall have historically aligned price adjustments in late summer ahead of the Q4 specification season.

## What to Do Right Now

**If you have an active Fleetwood quote:** Check the expiration date immediately. Most Fleetwood quotes are valid for 30 days from issue. Anything quoted before mid-August is at risk of expiring before September 1st. Call your dealer and ask whether the quote can be extended or whether an order needs to be placed to lock pricing.

**If you're mid-design and Fleetwood is on the spec:** Get a preliminary quote now even if the design isn't fully finalized. A rough configuration quote locks you into pre-increase pricing while you finish details. This is standard practice with major manufacturer increases.

**If you're a contractor bidding a job:** Factor the new pricing into any bids you haven't submitted yet. A 7% increase on a $60,000 Fleetwood opening package is $4,200 — not a rounding error on a tight margin.

## The Competitive Picture

At the ultra-luxury aluminum tier, Fleetwood competes primarily with NanaWall, LaCantina (on folding), and Western Window Systems (on multi-slide). After this increase, the price gap between Fleetwood and those competitors narrows slightly — particularly against LaCantina's aluminum folding system, which has been gaining ground on Fleetwood specs in the $500k+ custom home segment.

For homeowners and architects who have been on the fence between Fleetwood and NanaWall for a folding door application, now is the time to run a side-by-side quote on both before September pricing takes effect on either line.

## San Diego Market Note

Fleetwood is manufactured in El Monte, California — about 90 miles from San Diego — which has historically given SD dealers favorable lead times compared to out-of-state competitors. Current lead times are running 8–10 weeks on standard residential configurations, 12–14 weeks on custom sizes. Order now if you want delivery well ahead of the increase.

Local dealers including OrepAC and American Building Supply will have more specific guidance on which configurations are most affected and whether any current stock can be purchased at pre-increase pricing.
